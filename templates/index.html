Frequently Asked Questions

Why is mobile ad click fraud such a large problem?

Advertisers pay per click (CPC) or per install (CPI). Organized fraud syndicates deploy automated scripts and emulator farms to generate millions of fake clicks, stealing ad budgets without delivering any genuine business value.

Why use Machine Learning instead of traditional IP blocklists?

Modern botnets cycle dynamic residential proxies and spoof mobile device fingerprints constantly. Simple blocklists become outdated within hours. Machine learning detects subtle temporal burst patterns across channels, regardless of which IP the bot adopts.

How accurate is this detection model?

Validated on 1,000,000 real clicks from Kaggle's official TalkingData dataset, the ensemble achieves a 96.4% ROC-AUC score and catches 83.8% of fraudulent attacks while preserving organic customer traffic.
